# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** To be confirmed
**Last updated:** November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the School Board team ("School Board," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use School Board — currently displayed in the app as "Campus Connect" — including its website and any related app (the "Service"). It is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). [Legal entity name to be confirmed.]

## 1. Summary

- We collect the information you provide to create an account (name, username, school email, role, department, graduation year, password) and the content you post, upload, and message.
- Other members see your posts, comments, events, and listings under an anonymous alias, not your name. We know which account created each item, authorized administrators can view that information, and we may disclose it where the law requires or to protect safety.
- We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics trackers.
- We use service providers — Supabase, Vercel, and Google — to run the Service.

## 2. Information we collect

### Information you provide

| Category | Data |
|---|---|
| Account | Full name, username, school email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider) |
| Academic affiliation | Role (Current Student, Alumni, Faculty, Staff), department or major, graduation year (optional) |
| Profile | Bio, profile photo |
| Posts and comments | Post titles, text, images, category; comments; likes; previous versions of posts you edit |
| Events | Title, date and time, venue, description, image, category, optional location (place name, address, coordinates); RSVPs, likes, comments |
| Marketplace | Listing title, category, price, condition, description, images, optional location; saves and comments |
| Direct messages | Message text and images, sender display name, read status, and the listing or event a conversation is about |
| Reports and blocks | What you reported, the reason and any details you add; the members you have blocked |
| Communications | Anything you send to us |

### Information collected automatically

- **Timestamps** for accounts, content, messages, and reports.
- **Sign-in data** stored in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.
- **Technical logs** kept by our hosting and database providers, which may include IP addresses and browser information, as part of operating their services.

### Information from others

- Other members may report content you created.
- When you search for a location, Google returns place details (name, address, coordinates), which are saved with your event or listing.

### What we do not ask for

We do not request your precise device location, contacts, payment information, or government ID. Please do not include sensitive information (for example, health information or financial account numbers) in posts or messages.

## 3. Who can see what

| Information | Other members | School Board administrators |
|---|---|---|
| Your name and email | Not displayed on posts, comments, events, or listings. In direct messages, the other participant may see your account name. | Can view, to review sign-ups and reports, enforce our Terms, protect safety, and comply with law |
| Your posts, comments, events, listings | Shown with an anonymous alias | Can view, including reported or hidden content |
| Direct messages | Visible to the participants in the conversation | May be accessed where necessary to investigate a report, protect safety, or comply with law |

Direct messages are **not end-to-end encrypted**. Images you upload to posts, events, listings, and your profile are stored at web addresses that anyone with the link can open.

## 4. How we use information

We use personal information to:

1. **Provide the Service** — create and maintain your account, display content, deliver messages, and show events and listings.
2. **Verify school affiliation** — send and check a one-time email code and, for non-academic email addresses, have an administrator review your application.
3. **Keep the community safe** — review reports, hide or remove content, investigate violations, enforce blocks, prevent spam, and detect and report child sexual exploitation.
4. **Communicate with you** — send verification and password-reset emails and respond to your requests.
5. **Secure and maintain the Service** — fix problems and protect against fraud and abuse.
6. **Comply with law** — respond to valid legal process and meet legal reporting obligations.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, and we do not sell it.

## 5. How we share information

- **With other members** — the content you post (under an alias), your messages to the people you message, and any location you choose to add to an event or listing.
- **With service providers** that process data on our behalf:

| Provider | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, sign-in and email verification, file storage, real-time messaging | Account and content data |
| Vercel | Website hosting | Request data such as IP address and browser information |
| Google Maps and Places | Location search and maps for events and listings | Text typed into location search, IP address, and browser information ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)) |
| Google Fonts | Loading fonts | IP address and browser information |

- **For safety and legal reasons** — to comply with law or legal process; to report apparent child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2258A; to protect the rights, safety, or property of members, the public, or School Board; and to enforce our Terms.
- **Business transfers** — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.
- **With your consent** or at your direction.

We do not share your information with your college or university unless required by law or with your consent.

## 6. Retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed for the purposes above. **Specific retention periods are being finalized and will be stated here.** Currently:

- When your account is deleted, your profile and the posts, comments, likes, events, listings, saves, RSVPs, and blocks associated with it are deleted.
- Messages you sent may remain visible to the people you sent them to.
- Copies of uploaded images may not be removed immediately.
- Records of reports, including a copy of the reported content, and records of moderation actions are kept for safety purposes.
- Content reported to NCMEC is preserved as required by law.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Access and correct** — view and edit your profile (name, role, department, graduation year, bio, photo) in Edit Profile.
- **Delete content** — delete your own posts, comments, events, and listings, and delete conversations.
- **Delete your account** — contact us using the details in Section 12.
- **Request a copy** of your information — contact us using the details in Section 12.
- **Block** members and manage your blocked list in Edit Profile → Privacy & safety.

We will verify requests by confirming control of your account email.

### California residents

Depending on whether the California Consumer Privacy Act applies to us, California residents may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to delete it; to correct it; to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" (we do neither); to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 12.

**Do Not Track.** The Service does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals because we do not track users across third-party websites, and we do not allow third parties to collect information about your activity across websites through the Service.

## 8. Children

The Service is intended for college and university community members and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have, we will delete the account and information, except anything we are legally required to preserve. **Our minimum age requirement is under review and will be stated in our [Terms of Service](/terms).** See also our [Child Safety Standards](/child-safety).

## 9. Security

We use measures designed to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored only as hashes by our authentication provider, and access rules that limit what each account can see. No system is completely secure, and the Service is in beta. If we learn of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

## 10. Where we process data

We are based in the United States and process information in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. If changes are material, we will notify you before they take effect.
