# Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** To be confirmed
**Last updated:** November 22, 2026

School Board is a place for college and university communities to talk openly — including anonymously. Anonymity makes honest conversation possible, but it also makes it easy to hurt people. These Guidelines apply to everything on School Board: posts, comments, events, marketplace listings, profile photos and bios, and direct messages. They are part of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

**The short version:** be honest, be kind, respect privacy, keep it legal, and never involve minors in anything sexual.

## 1. What we encourage

- Campus news, questions, advice, and discussion — including criticism of ideas, institutions, and public conduct.
- Events described accurately.
- Fair, honest marketplace listings.
- Disagreement that targets arguments, not people.
- Helping others and reporting things that look wrong.

## 2. What is not allowed

### Child sexual abuse and exploitation — zero tolerance

Any content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children is prohibited, including sexualized images or text involving anyone under 18, grooming, sextortion, and attempts to arrange contact with minors. We remove it, terminate the account, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). See our [Child Safety Standards](/child-safety).

### Harassment and bullying

Do not target a person or group with abuse: insults or degrading comments, repeated unwanted contact, encouraging others to pile on, sexual harassment, mocking someone's appearance, or posting about someone to humiliate them. Posts that rate or "burn" identifiable classmates, faculty, or staff are not allowed. Contacting someone who has blocked you or asked you to stop — including through another account — is harassment.

### Hate speech

Do not attack or dehumanize people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, caste, religion, sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, serious disease, age, or immigration status. This includes slurs, harmful stereotypes, and hateful symbols. Discussing or condemning hate in an educational or news context is allowed when the purpose is clear.

### Violence and threats

No threats of violence against anyone, including "jokes" about attacking a campus; no glorifying violent attacks; no encouraging self-harm or suicide. If someone is in immediate danger, **call 911 or campus police first**, then report it on School Board.

If you are struggling, you can call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, U.S.).

### Privacy violations and de-anonymization

- Do not post anyone's private information without consent: real names tied to anonymous posts, phone numbers, home or dorm addresses, class schedules, student ID numbers, private accounts, financial or medical information, or images of someone in a private setting.
- **Do not try to unmask other members** — no guessing, speculating about, or publicizing who is behind an alias.
- Do not share private messages or screenshots of them to shame or expose someone.
- Never post intimate images of anyone without their consent.

### Impersonation and misrepresentation

Do not pretend to be another person, a school official, a student organization, or School Board staff. Do not sign up with someone else's school email or misrepresent your role or affiliation. Parody must be clearly labeled. Do not post misleading content about emergencies, safety, or official school announcements.

### Scams, spam, and fraud

- No fake listings, bait-and-switch, advance-payment or overpayment scams, fake housing or sublet offers, phishing links, or requests for passwords or verification codes.
- No repetitive or unsolicited promotion, pyramid schemes, or mass messaging.
- No selling or trading accounts or access to School Board.

### Sexual content

No pornography, sexually explicit images or descriptions, or sexual solicitation. Non-graphic discussion of sexual health, relationships, or consent is allowed.

### Prohibited marketplace items

You may not list: weapons, ammunition, or explosives; illegal drugs, drug paraphernalia, or prescription medications; alcohol, tobacco, vaping, or cannabis products; stolen or counterfeit goods; academic dishonesty services (writing assignments, taking exams, selling exam answers); adult sexual services; personal data or accounts; recalled or hazardous items; or anything illegal to sell where you or the buyer are located.

### Illegal and dangerous activity

Do not use School Board to plan or promote crime, obtain illegal goods or services, share instructions for causing serious harm, or promote dangerous challenges or hazing.

### Intellectual property

Only post content you have the right to share. Do not post copyrighted course materials, exams, or textbooks without permission.

### Abusing the platform

No fake or duplicate accounts, block evasion, automated posting, scraping, or attempts to bypass the app's security, rate limits, or moderation. Do not file false or bad-faith reports.

## 3. Reporting and blocking

### Reporting

Tap the **⋯** menu on a post, comment, event, marketplace listing, or comment and choose **Report**. Pick the reason that fits best and add details if helpful. Reports go to School Board administrators, not to the person you reported, and the person you report is not told who reported them.

Report reasons: Spam · Harassment · Hate speech · Sexual content · Violence or threats · Personal information · Impersonation · Other.

- **Child safety concerns:** choose "Sexual content" and also contact us as described in our [Child Safety Standards](/child-safety).
- **Direct messages or member accounts:** these cannot yet be reported from inside the app. Contact us using the details in our [Terms of Service](/terms) and describe the conversation or member.

### Blocking

Choose **Block** from the same menu. When you block someone:

- you won't see each other's posts, comments, events, or listings;
- neither of you can like, comment on, save, or RSVP to the other's content;
- neither of you can start a new conversation, and existing conversations become read-only;
- they are not notified.

Manage blocked members in **Edit Profile → Privacy & safety → Blocked users**.

## 4. How moderation works

1. **Reports are reviewed** by School Board administrators. We may also act on content we find ourselves.
2. **Context matters.** We consider the content, intent, whether the person targeted is identifiable, whether it is newsworthy or educational, and the member's history.
3. **Possible actions on content:** no action; **hide** (the content is no longer shown to members and can be restored); or **remove** (deleted). A copy of reported content is kept with the report for review.
4. **Account review.** For serious reports, administrators may look up the account behind content to investigate violations, protect safety, or comply with law.
5. **Moderation actions are recorded** internally.

If you believe we made a mistake, contact us using the details in our [Terms of Service](/terms) and ask for a review.

## 5. Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, we may remove content, restrict or terminate accounts, and — for child sexual exploitation and other serious harms — report to NCMEC or law enforcement where required. We may skip steps for serious violations, and accounts created to evade enforcement will be removed.

## 6. Changes

We may update these Guidelines as the community grows. Material changes will be announced in the app.
