## 1. Our commitment

School Board has **zero tolerance** for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We prohibit any content or conduct that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children, whether it happens on our service or is arranged through it. These Standards apply to all users, all content, and all features — posts, comments, events, marketplace listings, profile photos, and direct messages — regardless of the age of our users.

School Board is intended for college and university community members and is not directed to children. Our minimum age requirement is under review and will be stated in our [Terms of Service](/terms).

## 2. What is prohibited

The following are prohibited and will result in removal of the content, termination of the account, and reporting to authorities as required by law:

1. **CSAM** — any visual depiction (photo, video, drawing, AI-generated or computer-generated image, or edited image) of a minor engaged in sexually explicit conduct or that sexualizes a minor, including sharing links to or requesting such material.
2. **Sexualization of minors** — sexualized text, comments, or role-play about minors, or sexualized commentary on images of minors.
3. **Grooming** — building a relationship with a minor in order to sexually exploit or abuse them, including moving a minor to another platform for that purpose.
4. **Sextortion** — threatening to share intimate images of a minor, or coercing a minor into producing sexual content, money, or anything else.
5. **Trafficking** — advertising, soliciting, or facilitating the commercial sexual exploitation of a minor.
6. **Arranging contact** — using the service to arrange in-person or online sexual contact with a minor.
7. **Any other content or behavior** that sexually exploits or endangers children.

## 3. How to report

**In the app.** Open the **⋯** menu on a post, comment, event, or listing and choose **Report**, then select **Sexual content** and describe the concern. Also contact us below so the report is prioritized.

**Direct messages and accounts.** These cannot yet be reported from inside the app. Contact us below with the conversation, alias, and a description.

**By email.** Child safety contact: [email to be announced]. Describe the content and where it appears. **Do not attach, forward, or copy illegal images.**

**To authorities.** You can report directly to the **NCMEC CyberTipline** at [report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org/) or 1-800-843-5678. **If a child is in immediate danger, call 911.**

## 4. How we respond

1. **Priority review.** Child safety reports are reviewed ahead of other reports.
2. **Restriction and removal.** Content that appears to involve CSAE is hidden from all users and removed once confirmed.
3. **Account action.** We terminate the account responsible for confirmed CSAE.
4. **Reporting to NCMEC.** When we obtain actual knowledge of apparent CSAM or child sexual exploitation, we report it to the NCMEC CyberTipline as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2258A.
5. **Preservation.** We preserve reported material as required by law, with access restricted to designated personnel, and cooperate with law enforcement requests made through valid legal process.
6. **No redistribution.** Our team does not copy, forward, or share suspected CSAM except as required to report it.
7. **Underage users.** If we learn an account belongs to a child, we close it. If there are signs that a minor is at risk, we treat it as a child safety report.

## 5. Prevention

- **School email verification.** Accounts require verification of an academic email address or review by an administrator.
- **Blocking.** Members can block other members, which stops new conversations and makes existing ones read-only.
- **Clear rules.** Our [Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines) and [Terms of Service](/terms) prohibit CSAE, and everyone with moderation access is expected to follow these Standards.
